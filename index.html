    FAQ

    Helpful answers.

    Is FixSend AI-powered?

    The free MVP uses rule-based analysis. AI-powered deep reports may come later.

    Do I need an account?

    No.

    Is my CV stored?

    Free analysis runs locally in your browser. If you choose Fix Pack, your CV is used to create a private paid dashboard and email you the link.

    Which roles is FixSend built for?

    FixSend is optimized for design, product, and creative tech roles such as UX, UI, product design, brand design, marketing design, frontend, and Webflow/no-code roles.

    Does my CV need to be in English?

    For the most accurate score, yes. The current keyword checks are built around English CV and role language, so non-English CVs may receive inaccurate results.

    Does this guarantee interviews?

    No. It helps you identify gaps before applying.
